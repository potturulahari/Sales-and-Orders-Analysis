# 📊 Sales and Orders Analysis Using Excel

## 📌 About This Project

This is one of my data analysis projects, where I used Microsoft Excel to explore sales and order data.

I worked with a dataset containing information about sales, profits, discounts, products, regions, and customer segments.

My main goal was to understand how sales were performing across different regions, identify profitable and loss-making products, and explore sales trends over time.

Through this project, I gained practical experience in analyzing data using Excel and presenting the results in a simple and understandable way.

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Excel Charts
- Data Analysis

## 📊 What I Analyzed

**1. Region-Wise Sales Analysis**

I compared sales across different regions to understand which regions contributed the most to overall sales.

**2. Profit Analysis by Category**

I analyzed profits across Furniture, Office Supplies, and Technology to understand which categories generated higher profits.

**3. Segment-Wise Sales Analysis**

I explored sales across Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments to understand their contributions to total sales.

**4. Monthly Sales Trends**

I analyzed monthly sales to identify changes in sales performance throughout the year.

**5. Top 5 Products by Sales**

I identified the five products that generated the highest sales.

**6. Top 5 Loss-Making Products**

I also analyzed products with negative profits to understand which products were contributing to losses.

## 💡 Key Findings

Some interesting findings from my analysis were:

- Total sales were approximately 12.64 million.
- Total profit was approximately 1.47 million.
- Technology generated the highest profit among the three product categories.
- The Consumer segment contributed the highest sales.
- The analysis helped identify both top-selling products and products generating losses.
